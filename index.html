Questions

Frequently asked questions

How accurate is my instant quote?
Our calculator reflects the actual prices we charge every day. In the vast majority of appointments, your final invoice matches the quote exactly. If we arrive and find something unexpected — like severe pet hair matting or a stain that needs a specialty product — we'll tell you before we touch the vehicle.
Do I have to book after getting a quote?
Not at all. The calculator is there so you can see real pricing without having to call us. No credit card, no commitment. If you'd like to move forward, just submit the form and we'll reach out to confirm.
Can I change my selections after submitting?
Yes, easily. When we call or text to confirm, just tell us what you'd like to change and we'll adjust the quote on the spot. You can also text us at the business phone number any time before your appointment.
What areas do you service?
We cover Austin and the immediate surrounding cities: Round Rock, Cedar Park, Pflugerville, Buda, and Kyle. If you're just outside that range, reach out — we handle out-of-area jobs on a case-by-case basis, typically with a small travel fee already reflected in the calculator.
How quickly will I hear back?
Within one business hour, Monday through Friday 7:30 AM – 6 PM, and Saturday 8 AM – 4 PM. Requests submitted Sunday or after-hours are answered first thing the next morning — usually before 8 AM.
What payment methods do you accept?
Cash, all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover), Apple Pay, Venmo, and Zelle. Payment is collected after the service is complete and you're satisfied — never before.
